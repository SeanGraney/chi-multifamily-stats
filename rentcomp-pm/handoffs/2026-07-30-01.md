## ⚠ THE 529 EPISODE — and the operational lesson worth keeping

All four agents died within seconds of spawn: **`API Error: 529 Overloaded`**,
server-side and transient. Each had read some docs and done nothing else.

**What worked: `SendMessage` to the agent, which resumed it from its transcript
with its context intact — no re-dispatch, no re-reading, no re-spent tokens.**
Note the exact mechanics, because the obvious call fails:

- `SendMessage` **by the name I assigned at spawn returned "not reachable"**
  after the failure.
- `SendMessage` **by the `agentId` from the spawn result worked**, and the
  result read *"had no active task; resumed from transcript."*

⭐ **So: keep the `agentId` of every agent you spawn.** After a mid-run death
the name may not resolve but the ID still does, and resuming beats re-spawning
by a wide margin. A dead agent is not necessarily a lost agent.

**If a wave of agents dies simultaneously on 529, suspect capacity, not your
dispatch.** Four identical failures in one second is a server, not four
coincidences. Resume; do not rewrite the dispatch.
